## On Motivation

There is a romance to doing impossible things. You can't fail because no one has done it before anyway. People don't have expectations because they think it is impossible. Being one person who believes in a sea of disbelievers.

Doing hard things is fulfilling. The learning that it accompanies keeps you fresh. Past experiences have though me that I thrive on learning and impact.

## On Work

Approach work with an objective frame of mind. Separate people from feedback. Accept, reject, criticize and challenge the feedback. Always try to do the right thing. Speed is useless if you trade it off with "doing the right thing".

Accept your limits. Define them and clearly communicate them. A day has 24 hours. This is a fact that won't change for you. Do you work with rigor and utmost honesty. But at the same time set clear expectations. If something new has to be prioritized, some existing work will get de prioritized.

## On Design

Separate people from feedback. Accept, reject, criticize and challenge the feedback. Always try to do the right thing. Speed is useless if you trade it off with "doing the right thing".

Try to learn more. Seek to understand where people are coming from before you challenge them. Challenge respectfully. Don't speak over someone.
