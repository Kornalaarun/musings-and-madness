---
title: "The Stepping Stone Fallacy"
description: "On missing the opportunities in front of you while looking ahead."
date: 2026-09-17
originalDate: 2022-05-29
topics: ["bites", "reflections"]
---

I always thought the current work was just a stepping stone and all my glories lied a future work which would showcase my talents and help me become successful.

So why stress about a stepping stone? Why give it your all?

The fallacy here is that the opportunities were always there in the current moment and I was unable to see them because I was too busy looking ahead.
