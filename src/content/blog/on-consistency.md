Consistent sustainable efforts compound in the long run. There is so much to learn that you will never know everything. Increasing the no of hours will not change that, it will just ramp up stress. Blaming yourself will not change that, if anything it will take away your time.

Learning is a continuous process. With finite time and finite energy, there a finite no of things you will be good at. Let that sink in. And it's not a lack of skill, it's a lack of time.

The constant context switching is a recipe for disaster. You will never master everything, but in attempting it you will also end up never mastering anything.

What to do then is the logical question.

Here are some thoughts :
- Learn consistently but don't overdo it. You have limited hours and limited energy. Allocate them wisely.
- Be very picky on subjects you want to explore in depth.
- Be okay with not knowing things. Learning is acknowledging ignorance regularly and being okay with it.
- Don't take yourself too seriously. You are a blip on the cosmic scale.
- Help people with what you learn.
