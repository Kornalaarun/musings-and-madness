---
title: "On Debates and Disagreements"
description: "Quick notes on how to argue well."
date: 2026-09-17
originalDate: 2022-09-10
topics: ["bites", "reflections"]
---

- Debates are a collaborative exercise and not a competitive exercise.
- The longer you argue the more stubborn you become.
- Distrust your first instinct to argue.
- Look for areas of agreement and establish common ground.
- Be honest and accept your mistakes.
- Control your temper.
- Welcome the disagreement.
- Promise to think over your opponents ideas and study them.
- Thank your opponents sincerely for their interests.
- Postpone action to give both sides the time to think.
