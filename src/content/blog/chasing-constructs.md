Everyone is chasing something. Independent of what people are chasing and the intensity, at a fundamental level the chase is the same.

Do I want this? People are fundamentally chasing constructs. They are just an extension of your acquired identity. Is it possible to grow without taking on an identity?

Is it possible to chase with a more universal identity. Chasing titles causes identity crisis once you switch companies or job functions. However creating value and impact are universal values. Chasing them and identifying with them might be a longer lasting relationship.
