What is my story? What do I want from life? I have a lot of things most people aspire for and I am grateful for it. Of course I want to earn more money, it would be dishonest to convince myself otherwise. But that is not a strong motivator. It does not help me go to bed happy or motivate me to wake up early.

I love learning things. I thrive on making the world bend to my will by relying solely on my knowledge and skills. I want to be the best version of myself that I can be. I want to continuously learn from project, people and situations. I want to add value to every single interaction I have with people. I take pride in doing a good job and being acknowledged for it.

Be optimistic. People are mostly good most of the times. Trust people. Develop genuine human relationships, that's going to be your rebellion against cold hearted capitalism. Care about the other person doing well. Of all the people who exist in the world, you will know less than 0.00001% of them. If you elevate those people you get elevated by default. When in doubt prioritize human connection and well being over everything else.

Live a life free of addiction in any shape or form. Have zero sunk cost. Start from scratch again and again. The more you do it the better you get at it. Temper your lifestyle. Be persistent but at the same time be forgiving. Life is full of joy and abundance. Everything happens for a reason. And the thing is you don't really need to know the reason. Be 💯% involved and experience everything. Good bad and ugly. Live through it all and tell stories to help everyone who comes after you. Nothing is final, except death.
