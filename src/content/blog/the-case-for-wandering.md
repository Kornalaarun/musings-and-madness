All of us have a tall list of things we want to do, things we want to learn, things we want to read and so on. Being ambitious is not at all bad. However your efficient lists will only take you so far.

Wandering is an essential counter-balance to efficiency. You need to employ both. The outsized discoveries – the "non-linear" ones – are highly likely to require wandering. -- Jeff Bezos

Wandering is wonderful because it leads you to places where your lists wouldn't. Because your lists manifest something you know and are biased by your notions of what are important and unimportant. Wandering on the other hand takes you places you didn't know existed and thus help expand your worldview.

In a 24 hour day that is already strained with work and all of the things that you want to learn how do you find time to wander?

Wandering is time consuming. You cannot and should not wander if you are on the clock or chasing a deadline. The outrageous problems, the unsolvable ones, personal projects, non work work are things which don't have hard deadlines.

So what next? Go, wander.
